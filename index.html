Care & Craft

Four steps, every strap
01

Cut

Measured on a marked bench and cut to length with a hot knife, so the sheath can't fray.

02

Seal

Each end is fused with heat, then whipped tight with waxed thread.

03

Knot

A double fisherman's knot, dressed and set by hand. It slides when you move it and holds when you don't.

04

Test

5 kg hung for 24 hours with knot slip measured, and 50 open-close cycles on every clip.

Care

  • Wipe clean with a damp cloth. For a deeper clean, hand wash in cool water and dry flat.
  • Keep away from open flame and high heat. Polyester melts.
  • Check the clips and knot before carrying anything heavy.
  • Decorative carry strap. Not for climbing.
